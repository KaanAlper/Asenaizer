DESKTOP APP

Run Asenaizer on your computer

A small tray app runs the model on your GPU. The model loads only while this site is connected.

Download for Windows ↓

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Setup downloads the newest version, checks it and installs it for your account. No administrator rights needed.

Paste this into a terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KaanAlper/Asenaizer/main/scripts/install-linux.sh | bash

Works on Debian, Ubuntu, Arch, Fedora and openSUSE. Missing system libraries are installed for you, and the app opens in your tray when it finishes.

After installing, come back and select Connect this device.
